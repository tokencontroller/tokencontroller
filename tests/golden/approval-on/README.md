# Approval on

From the docs pages [Approval](https://docs.tokencontroller.com/account/approval) and [Monthly close](https://docs.tokencontroller.com/account/close).

Kestrel Digital switches approval on. At the end of October:

| # | Token entry | Status | In reports | On the cost sheet |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mara on KD-214, 20.00 | approved | yes | yes |
| 2 | Lena on KD-231, 15.00 | submitted, not yet approved | yes | no |
| 3 | Lena, 5.00 | rejected by her people manager | no | no |
| 4 | Tom's review bot on KD-214, 3.00 | approved: automatic entries are approved the same way | yes | yes |
| 5 | Mara, 4.00 | draft, not submitted | no | no |

Submitted cost counts in reports at once, so reports show 38.00. The cost sheet holds approved cost only: 23.00 against a bill of 50.00, so 27.00 is residual until the rest is approved.

The close check finds two entries that are not approved (2 and 3), so the period cannot close yet.

What it checks: which statuses count in reports and on the cost sheet, and that unapproved and rejected entries stop the close.
