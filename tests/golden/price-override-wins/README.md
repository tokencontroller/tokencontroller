# The price table override wins

The managed table prices input at 10 per million from 1 January and at 20 from 11 March. The organization overrides input to 5 from 10 March. S1 on 2 March costs 400 at the managed price. S4 on 12 March, 15 million input tokens, costs 75 at the override, not 300 at the newer managed row: the override wins while it is valid. Total 475.
