On the Enterprise plan, members can sign in with your identity provider, and SCIM keeps the member list in step.

OIDC

Under Settings, Sign-in, add your provider's issuer URL, client ID and secret. Any OIDC provider works, such as Entra ID, Okta or Google Workspace.

SCIM

Create a SCIM token under Settings and give it with the SCIM URL to your identity provider. People you add or remove there are added or removed as members. Their role and tags are set in Token Controller.
