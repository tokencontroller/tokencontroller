An organization is one customer of Token Controller: its people, groups, projects and periods. It has one reporting currency.

Organization settings

SettingWhat it does
Cost sourceHow your provider charges you. It decides what the close reconciles against; see Bills.
Attribution targetThe share of cost you aim to place, and how specific a placement must be to count: on a project, or on a ticket. Default 80 percent, on a ticket.
Reporting currencyThe one currency the organization computes in. Bills in other currencies are booked at the exchange rate of their date.
ApprovalOn or off. See Approval.
Sign-inWhich sign-in providers members may use, and which email domains.

Groups

A group is a label on people: a team, a department, a discipline such as engineering or design. A person can carry several. Reports total by group, and a people manager can be assigned groups. Token Controller does not model your org chart, and groups carry no token budget: budgets sit on people, tickets, projects and periods.

Groups are separate from projects on purpose: one person can work on several projects whatever groups they are in.

When someone moves

When a person changes group or leaves, only future token entries follow. Past postings keep the groups they had, so last month's reports do not change.
