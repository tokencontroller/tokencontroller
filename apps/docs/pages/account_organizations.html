An organization is one customer of Token Controller: its people, projects, tags and periods. A controller sets it up once.

Organization settings

SettingWhat it does
Cost sourceHow your provider charges you. It decides what the close reconciles against; see Bills.
Reporting currencyThe one currency the organization computes in. Bills in other currencies are booked at the exchange rate of their date.
Tag names and tag mapThe tag names your organization uses, which tag is the cost center and the ladder for the cost object. See Tags and the tag map.
Ticket systemsWhere tickets sync from. See Projects and tickets.
Organization rulesRules that run before everyone's personal rules. See Rules.
ApprovalOn or off. See Approval.
Sign-inWhich sign-in providers members may use, and which email domains.

People

Invite people by email. Each gets a role and their tags, such as team: web. When a person changes team or leaves, only their future token entries follow; last month's reports do not change.
