An organization is one customer of Token Controller: its people, projects, tags and periods. It has one reporting currency.

Organization settings

SettingWhat it does
Cost sourceHow your provider charges you. It decides what the close reconciles against; see Bills.
Attribution targetThe share of cost you aim to place. Default 80 percent. Which tags must be filled to count is part of the tag map.
Reporting currencyThe one currency the organization computes in. Bills in other currencies are booked at the exchange rate of their date.
ApprovalOn or off. See Approval.
Tag names and tag mapThe tag names your organization uses, which tag is the cost center, the ladder for the cost object, and the required tags. See Tags and the tag map.
Sign-inWhich sign-in providers members may use, and which email domains.

Teams and departments

Teams, departments and disciplines are tags on people, such as team: web or discipline: design. A person has one value per tag name; someone in two teams picks a main one, or the organization uses a second tag name such as guild. Token entries inherit the person's tags, so reports, token budgets and people managers' access work by team like by any other tag. Token Controller does not model your org chart.

Teams are separate from projects on purpose: one person can work on several projects whatever team they are in.

When someone moves

When a person changes team or leaves, only future token entries follow. Past token entries keep the tags they had, so last month's reports do not change.
