A project is a body of work made of tickets. A client is a tag on a project.

What a project has

  • a ticket key pattern, such as ^KD-\d+
  • its repositories
  • tags, such as client: acme, which its token entries inherit
  • optionally a token budget and a cadence

Clients

A client is a tag on projects. Tag several projects client: acme and their token entries inherit it, so cost per client is a filter. An agency that wants client as a level of its own adds client as a rung of the ladder, above project.

Cost that belongs to no ticket

Project work that belongs to no single ticket, such as research or setup, is project overhead. Work that belongs to no project at all is organization overhead.
