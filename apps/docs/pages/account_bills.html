A bill is a provider's figure for one period's metered usage: gross, discount, credit and net. Metered usage is everything priced per use: tokens, and per-call fees such as web search. Token entries must add up to it. Seats, subscriptions and other flat fees are not usage: they stay off the bill and off the cost sheet, and finance handles them as before.

A provider can restate a bill. Each bill keeps its revisions. A bill in another currency than your reporting currency keeps its original amount and the exchange rate of its date.

Reconciliation

For each period and each bill: postings plus unattributed plus residual equal the bill. With several providers, each bill is checked on its own and the cost sheet adds them up to the organization total. Residual is the part of the bill no token entry explains: usage in the provider's own chat app, per-call fees the agent harness does not report, a restated bill. It is always shown.

List value and billed value

Every token entry has a list value: the agent's reported cost where it has one (Claude Code does), otherwise its token counts times the price table for the model. A billed value exists where a bill or billed telemetry says what was actually charged. Every figure is labelled billed, list-price estimate, or unknown model.
