Attribution is the share of cost whose required tags are filled, such as a project or a ticket. The attribution target is the share your organization aims for.

The default

80 percent, with project as the required tag. Expect 70 to 80 percent at first. It rises as you add rules and people fix exceptions.

What counts

A token entry counts as attributed when every required tag has a value, or when its cost kind says it belongs to no project: personal and organization overhead count. Choose the required tags in the tag map: the rung project, the rung ticket, or more, such as purpose.

Where you see it

On the rules page, in reports, in the Claude Code mod's band, and on the cost sheet. Whatever is not attributed is still counted: all of the bill is always accounted for, with unattributed as its own line.
