The cost sheet is the locked account of one closed period for the whole organization: cost per project and ticket, adding up to the bill.

What it shows

  • cost per rung of your ladder, by default per project and ticket, by cost kind
  • organization overhead and personal cost
  • unattributed and residual, each as its own line
  • adjustments posted after the close
  • the reconciliation against the bill

With approval on, it holds approved cost only.

For finance

A controller hands finance the cost sheet as a link or as CSV. The CSV can add any tag name as a column, such as team or purpose. Finance needs no login.

For your clients

Token Controller ends here. Charging a client for what is on the cost sheet happens in your own invoicing tool.
