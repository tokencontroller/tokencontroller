---
title: "Adjustments"
description: "Signed corrections to closed periods."
---

A closed period does not change. A mistake found later is corrected with an adjustment: a signed correction a controller posts to the closed period.

## Posting an adjustment {#post}

A controller chooses the period, the cost object, the amount (plus or minus) and a reason. The adjustment carries their name and the time.

## How it shows {#shows}

Adjustments are their own lines on the cost sheet and in reports. The original token entries and their tags stay as they were.
