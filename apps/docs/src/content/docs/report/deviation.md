---
title: "Deviation and forecast"
description: "Plan minus cost for any filter, labelled scope, trouble or the estimate itself, and the forecast as a report."
---

A deviation is plan minus cost, for any filter and date range. Positive is favourable. A forecast is where cost is heading. Both are reports over the same numbers, never records.

## Deviation {#deviation}

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Deviation of a filter over a date range | plan of the filter over the range − cost of the filter over the range |

Any filter: a feature, a project, a person, a department, a client. The plan comes from the [accepted estimate](/account/estimates#accept) and, for a person, from their [shares](/account/estimates#shares). The cost is the [one formula](/report/reports#formula).

| From the worked month | Plan | Cost | Deviation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Project Shop, March | 232.50 | 800 | −567.50, unfavourable |
| Feature KD-1 | 500 | 580 | −80, labelled scope |
| Person Mara, March | 139.50 | 700 | −560.50 |

Each row opens down to the sessions and their summaries. Look at both signs: an unfavourable deviation may be a problem, a favourable one may mean someone can teach the others, or that the next estimate should be lower.

## Labels {#labels}

The System 1 classifier reads the estimate's description and the sessions' summaries and labels why:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | More or less was built than described. |
| Trouble | Agents looped, failed or redid work. |
| Estimate itself | The work was as described and went well; the estimate was off. |

Each labelled deviation refits the estimator, so the next estimate for similar work is closer.

## Forecast {#forecast}

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Forecast of a filter for the next date range | computed from the cost of the filter over the past ranges |

A forecast is a report: ask for it on any filter, such as the organization, a team or a person, for the next month. Nothing is stored and nothing is sent. An estimate looks at one piece of work; a forecast looks at a filter over time.

## No alerts {#no-alerts}

A deviation sends no alert and stops nothing. Your provider offers spend caps.
