An outlier is a cost far from your organization's own baseline for similar work.

## The baseline {#baseline}

Token Controller compares each ticket with similar past tickets in your organization, not with other customers. Similar past tickets are the tickets of the same project in the twelve months before the period, or of the whole organization when the project has fewer than three. The baseline is their median cost. The report lists the tickets furthest from it, high or low: a ticket at twice the baseline is as far as one at half.

## What to do with it {#use}

Open the token entries and read the summaries. An outlier is a question to ask, not an alert: nothing is sent and nothing is stopped.
