title: "Raw export"
description: "Everything your organization has, as a zip of JSON."
---

Leaving must be easy. The raw export is everything your organization has in Token Controller, as a zip of JSON files.

## Get it {#get}

A controller chooses _Raw export_ under _Settings_. The zip holds every token entry, split, tag, bill, rule, ticket and period.

## Anything prettier {#enterprise}

Formatted exports beyond CSV and the raw export are part of the Enterprise plan.
