Enterprise plan instances are isolated: your organization alone, on its own instance.

## Hosted for you {#hosted}

An isolated hosted instance in the region you choose, with OIDC and SCIM, an audit log, telemetry passthrough to your own collector, cloud bill connectors on request, and a support SLA. Nothing is shared with other organizations: the instance has its own service and its own database.

## Self-hosted with support {#self}

A self-hosted instance with a support contract is an Enterprise plan instance. You keep your own Postgres and your own region; we send you what you need to switch the features on. [Talk to us](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jancbeck/).

## What is not open source {#features}

Enterprise features and our own billing and support tools are not open source. The Enterprise plan features sit on top of the open-source service, and nothing in the open-source version depends on them. If an Enterprise plan ends, your data stays: people sign in by magic link, and every report and export keeps working. See [Open source and licenses](/reference/open-source).
