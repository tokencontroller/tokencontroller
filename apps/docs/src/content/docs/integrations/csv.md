title: "CSV bills"
description: "Upload any bill as CSV."
---

Any bill can be uploaded as CSV, once per period. This is also the way for Bedrock, Vertex and Foundry.

## Upload {#upload}

Under _Bills_, choose _Upload CSV_. Give the period, gross, discount, credit, net and currency, or upload the cloud's own export. The upload shows what it read before anything is saved.

## Connectors {#enterprise}

On an Enterprise plan contract, a connector can pull Bedrock, Vertex or Foundry bills instead.
